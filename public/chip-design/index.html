From design to factory to package

Every box is a team. Every team is a job title.

Engineers design the chip in software. A fab prints it onto silicon wafers. Packaging plants cut, mount, connect and test each chip. India is building all three, and each stage hires engineers.

India, 2026

Micron's ATMP (assembly, test, marking and packaging) plant in Sanand, Gujarat opened on 28 February 2026 to assemble and test DRAM and NAND memory, with about 5,000 direct jobs expected at peak. Packaging plants from Kaynes, CG Power and Tata (Assam) are next. Packaging and test need engineers who understand both the chip and the physics, which is why the book covers analog, test and DFT as well as digital.

LAB D1 · DIGITAL · RTL DESIGN

A 4-bit counter, and the test that proves it

Count 0 to 15, wrap to 0, and restart cleanly when reset fires mid-count. The testbench checks every clock edge against a reference model.

$ vvp sim RESULT: PASS - reset and rollover verified (25 checks)

What it proves to a recruiter: you write clean sequential logic and you test edge cases, not just the easy path.

LAB D2 · DIGITAL · VERIFICATION

Find the hidden FIFO bug

A 4-entry buffer passes every normal test. Write five values into it and the buggy design silently overwrites the oldest one. One condition fixes it.

READ 50 expected 10 <-- MISMATCH RESULT: FAIL - 1 mismatches at the full boundary RESULT: PASS - full-boundary test passed (fixed)

Why it matters: this is the daily job of a verification engineer, the largest hiring track.

LAB A1 · ANALOG · CIRCUITS

An RC filter where theory meets simulation

R = 1 kΩ, C = 100 nF. The formula 1/(2πRC) predicts a 1,591.5 Hz cutoff. The simulator measured it.

$ ngspice -b rc_lowpass.cir fc = 1.591551e+03 (1,591.6 Hz)

Where it lives: in front of every microphone, touchscreen and sensor that feeds an analog-to-digital converter.

LAB A2 · ANALOG MEETS DIGITAL · PHYSICAL DESIGN

Inside a logic gate: the CMOS inverter

Every digital gate is an analog circuit. Balance the transistors and it switches at exactly half the supply. Make its load four times heavier and watch it slow down.

vm = 0.900 V tphl = 29.6 ps (20 fF load) tphl = 80.5 ps (80 fF load)

The bridge: this effect is what physical-design and timing engineers fight every day.

Questions

Before you sign up

Do I need paid tools like Cadence or Synopsys?

No. Every lab uses free software: Icarus Verilog, ngspice, Yosys and an open-source layout flow. The Verilog labs also run in a browser on EDA Playground, with nothing to install.

I'm in 2nd year. Is this too early?

It's a good time. The first phase covers digital logic, CMOS and Verilog basics, which line up with your coursework. Use the flexible pace of 5–7 hours a week.

Will this get me a job?

No book can promise that. It gives you a clear plan, real projects and practice for the questions companies ask. Your results depend on the work you put in and on each employer's needs.

Does it cover analog and packaging, or only digital?

All of them. Two of the four starter labs are analog, and the full book has chapters on analog and mixed-signal design, physical design and timing, DFT, test and post-silicon validation.
