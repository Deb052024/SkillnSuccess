The problem

Why so many business owners overpay for solar

01

Oversized systems

Panels you can't use in daylight, with no fair export rate, are capital you paid for and never get back.

02

Quotes built on the wrong rate

Divide your whole bill by your units and payback looks a year shorter than it really is. Fixed charges don't go away.

03

Money left on the table

Section 12B lets most businesses deduct 100% of a solar system in year one. Many owners never claim it.
